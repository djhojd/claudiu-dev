This is the first post on my new blog. I built it to have a small, quiet corner
of the internet that's mine — somewhere to think out loud, keep notes I'll want
later, and share the occasional thing I've figured out.

I expect to write mostly about the things I build and the problems I run into
along the way. No fixed schedule, no grand plan — just short posts when I have
something worth writing down.

More soon.
